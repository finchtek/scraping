Classic Chocolate Chip Cookies

Makes 24 cookies

Moderate
Prep: 15 mins | Cook: 10 mins

Ingredients

  • 225g (1 cup) unsalted butter, softened
  • 225g (1 cup) brown sugar
  • 100g (½ cup) white sugar
  • 2 large eggs
  • 2 tsp vanilla extract
  • 300g (2½ cups) all-purpose flour
  • 1 tsp baking soda
  • ½ tsp salt
  • 200g (1 cup) chocolate chips
  • 100g (½ cup) chopped walnuts (optional)

Method

  1. Preheat oven to 190°C (170°C fan). Line baking sheets with parchment paper.
  2. Cream together the softened butter, brown sugar, and white sugar until light and fluffy.
  3. Beat in the eggs one at a time, then stir in the vanilla extract.
  4. Mix the flour, baking soda, and salt in a separate bowl.
  5. Gradually stir the dry ingredients into the wet mixture until just combined.
  6. Fold in the chocolate chips (and chopped walnuts, if using).
  7. Drop rounded tablespoons of dough onto the prepared baking sheets.
  8. Bake for 9-11 mins until golden brown around the edges but still soft in the centre.
  9. Cool on the baking sheet for 5 mins, then transfer to a wire rack to cool completely.

Per cookie: 150 kcal, 8g fat, 20g carbs, 2g protein
