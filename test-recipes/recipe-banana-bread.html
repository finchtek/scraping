Easy Banana Bread

Makes 1 loaf

Easy
Prep: 10 mins | Cook: 50 mins

Ingredients

  • 3 ripe bananas
  • 100g unsalted butter, melted
  • 100g caster sugar
  • 2 large eggs
  • 1 tsp vanilla extract
  • 150g self-raising flour
  • ½ tsp baking soda
  • handful of walnuts, roughly broken (optional)

Method

  1. Preheat oven to 180°C (160°C fan). Grease a 900g loaf tin and line the base with parchment paper.
  2. Mash the ripe bananas in a bowl until smooth.
  3. Stir in the melted butter, sugar, eggs, and vanilla extract until well combined.
  4. Fold in the self-raising flour and baking soda (and walnuts, if using).
  5. Pour the mixture into the prepared loaf tin.
  6. Bake for 50-55 mins until golden and a skewer inserted into the centre comes out clean.
  7. Cool in the tin for 10 mins, then transfer to a wire rack to cool completely.

Per slice: 210 kcal, 8g fat, 32g carbs, 3g protein
