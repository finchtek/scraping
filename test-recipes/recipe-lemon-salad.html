Lemon Herb Salad

Serves 4

Easy
Prep: 10 mins | Cook: 0 mins

Ingredients

  • 2 cups mixed salad leaves (rocket, spinach, lettuce)
  • 1 large cucumber, sliced
  • 2 radishes, thinly sliced
  • 1 red onion, finely diced
  • 1 lemon, zested and juiced
  • 2 tbsp extra virgin olive oil
  • 1 tbsp fresh dill, chopped
  • 1 tbsp fresh mint, chopped
  • Salt and black pepper to taste
  • ½ feta cheese, crumbled (optional)

Method

  1. Wash and dry the salad leaves. Arrange them in a large serving bowl.
  2. Add the cucumber slices, radish slices, and red onion dice to the bowl.
  3. In a small bowl, whisk together the lemon zest, lemon juice, olive oil, dill, and mint.
  4. Season the dressing with salt and pepper to taste.
  5. Drizzle the dressing over the salad just before serving.
  6. Sprinkle with crumbled feta if using.

Per serving: 120 kcal, 8g fat, 6g carbs, 3g protein
