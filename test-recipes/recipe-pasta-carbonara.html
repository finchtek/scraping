Quick Pasta Carbonara

Serves 2

Easy
Prep: 5 mins | Cook: 10 mins

Ingredients

  • 200g spaghetti
  • 2 free-range eggs
  • 50g Parmesan cheese, grated
  • 50g Pancetta, diced
  • 1 clove garlic, crushed
  • Freshly ground black pepper
  • 2 tbsp olive oil

Method

  1. Cook the spaghetti in boiling salted water according to package directions, until al dente.
  2. While the pasta cooks, fry the pancetta in a pan with olive oil until crisp and golden.
  3. Whisk the eggs with the grated Parmesan and plenty of black pepper.
  4. Drain the spaghetti, reserving a ladle of pasta water.
  5. Tip the hot spaghetti into the pancetta pan, toss to combine, then remove from the heat.
  6. Quickly stir in the egg and cheese mixture, adding a splash of pasta water if needed to create a creamy sauce.
  7. Serve immediately, with extra Parmesan and black pepper.

Per serving: 520 kcal, 18g fat, 65g carbs, 20g protein
